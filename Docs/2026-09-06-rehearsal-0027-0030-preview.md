`*.supabase.co` host) and no service-role key existed, so the four `tests/live-rls.test.mjs` sections
were reproduced through the MCP connector's `execute_sql` on the branch:

  member rows created by the `0001` trigger with role `member`;

  `set_config('request.jwt.claims', …)`, which `auth.uid()` reads; preflight confirmed
  `auth.uid()` returned the poster's id;

- every expected refusal was caught in a plpgsql sub-block and its SQLSTATE and message logged;
- origin/destination were the first two active seeded `locations` by slug
  (`14th-st-and-constitution-ave`, `14th-st-and-g-st`).

refuses `DELETE`, and `no_show_reports` holds `NO ACTION` FKs onto `members`. `tests/live-rls.test.mjs`'s
own `deleteUser` hits the same wall and ignores it, which is why the branch already held 69 such users.
