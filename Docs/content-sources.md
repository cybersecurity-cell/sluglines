# Sluglines Content Sources

Operational information is published with an explicit source and freshness state. A source being listed here does not make every historical claim current.

## Source hierarchy

1. Government or transit-operator pages for facilities, closures, schedules, and rules.
2. Current on-site signage confirmed by an editor or community steward.
3. Corroborated community reports for short-lived operational changes.
4. Historical Sluglines material for background and discovery only.

## Publication states

- **Verified:** Confirmed against a current primary source or a dated on-site review.
- **Community reported:** Recently reported but not yet corroborated by a primary source.
- **Needs review:** Useful orientation whose current operation has not been confirmed.
- **Historical:** Retained for context and never presented as current instructions.

## Initial sources

| Source | Type | Permitted use | Required review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Virginia Department of Transportation park-and-ride information | Official | Facility identity, address, access, and official notices | Confirm page and date before marking a location verified |
| WMATA, OmniRide, Fairfax Connector, and other operator sites | Official | Current transit alternatives and service alerts | Link to the operator rather than copying timetables |
| Sluglines community archive | Historical | Discover location names, terminology, and facts to re-research | Rewrite all copy and independently verify operational claims |
| Community correction reports | Community | Identify possible changes and trigger editorial review | Never auto-publish; corroborate or label community-reported |

## Prohibited publication inputs

- Personal contact exports, app-user exports, database dumps, password hashes, or analytics rows.
- Claims based only on undated screenshots, social posts, or saved web pages.
- Exact wait times, rider volumes, safety records, or performance claims without current evidence.
- Competitor prose, testimonials, photographs, maps, or branded graphics without permission.
- Automated Facebook or WhatsApp collection.

## Editorial review checklist

- Identify the exact location, travel direction, and destination.
- Confirm whether the information is current, temporary, or historical.
- Record the primary URL and review date.
- Use original wording and distinguish observation from official instruction.
- Avoid publishing personal names, faces, plates, or contact details.
- Add an expiry date to time-sensitive advisories.
